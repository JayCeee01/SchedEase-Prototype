What SchedEase Does

One place for the data and decisions behind every class schedule.

SchedEase brings departments, programs, year levels, sections, courses, rooms, faculty, credentials, availability, and published schedules into a single web application.

01 Organized academic records

Keep the core scheduling details in one clean workspace.

02 Clear assignment checks

Review room, faculty, section, and credential details before publishing.

03 Ready-to-share schedules

Give admins, faculty, and students a simple place to view final timetables.

Key Features

Designed for real academic scheduling work.

01

Smart schedule generation

Create draft schedules that consider rooms, faculty availability, section conflicts, and class time ranges.

02

Credential checks

Confirm faculty members have the right qualifications before they are assigned to courses.

03

Calendar-style review

Review generated schedules in timetable and table views before approving or publishing.

04

CSV and PDF export

Download schedules for sharing, printing, or further review.

Built for Everyone
